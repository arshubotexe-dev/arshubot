Chat tumhari, rules bhi tumhare.

Arshubot gaali aur spam pe turant timeout deta hai. Kaunse words band karne hain aur kitni der ka timeout, ye tum tay karo.

Live chat

Bot ki settings

Jo badloge, wo neeche settings.json mein turant dikhega. Wahi file bot padhta hai.

Bot

Bot chalu hai.

Timeout kitni der ka

Har galti pe timeout badhta jata hai. Second mein likho, 10 se 86400 tak.

Band words

Chhote words (4 letters tak) poore word ki tarah match hote hain, taaki galat log na pakde jaayein. Lambe words kahin bhi milein to pakde jaate hain.

Kya pakadna hai

Jo band karoge, bot use nahi pakdega.

Ye log safe hain

Channel owner aur moderators hamesha safe hain. Baaki dosto ke channel ke naam yahan jodo.

Commands

Viewer command likhe to bot ye jawab deta hai. Ek command 5 second mein ek hi baar chalta hai.
