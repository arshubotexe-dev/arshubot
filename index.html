Moderate your stream at the speed of chat.

Arshubot watches your live chat and times out spam and abuse the instant it happens. You decide the banned words, the timeout lengths, and the commands — every streamer's chat is different.

Three steps, no server to babysit

Arshubot is a small script plus a settings file. Change the settings, the bot picks them up.

1

Configure

Set your banned words, timeouts and commands below, then export the settings file.

2

Connect

Point the bot at your live stream and make it a moderator on your channel.

3

Moderate

Arshubot reads every message and reacts — timeouts for abuse and spam, replies for commands.

Bot settings

Everything here writes straight into settings.json, the file the bot reads.

Bot status

Arshubot is active.

Timeout lengths

Each repeat offence steps up. Enter seconds, from 10 to 86400.

Banned words

Short words (4 letters or fewer) only match as a whole word, so real names and normal chat don't get caught. Longer words match anywhere.

What gets flagged

Turn off anything you don't want the bot catching.

Trusted viewers

Channel owner and moderators are always trusted. Add friends' channel names here too.

Commands

When a viewer types a command, the bot replies with this. Each command has a 5-second cooldown.
